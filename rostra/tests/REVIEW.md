VERDICT: As built, Viva is a good rehearsal of the question-and-follow-up structure and a poor rehearsal of speaking: typing is the default, the spoken mode marks unverified notes, and a live score plus labelled follow-ups tell the student how they are doing after every answer. The final grade is not yet fair (one answered question can earn Distinction, the AI can move the mark 12 points across a band, and a mostly wrong but fluent candidate passes), and the format universities are actually adopting, defending your own submitted work, is missing. The prompts are a sound base and most fixes are small, but I exercised the flow and prompts with a stubbed AI, so how a live model behaves under the proposed wording is untested.
